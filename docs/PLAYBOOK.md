## The assignment

Help a non-technical operator go from a starting point to a first sales conversation.

Not: generate business ideas.

Yes: offer, price, pitch, site, prospects, route.

Less than 25 minutes of blank-page work.

You will not have made money. You will not have proven the price. You will have something specific to say when you walk in tomorrow.

---

## 1. Start with a painful problem that sits close to revenue

Ask for something a non-technical person could start, sell to local businesses, and scale if it worked.

Five services worth considering:

1. Appointment recovery
2. Review growth
3. A local content engine
4. Estimate follow-up
5. A simple front-desk chat assistant

The first two usually win the walk-in because the owner already understands the leak.

Missed calls and ignored inquiries become lost appointments. Completed jobs without review requests become lost reputation and future demand.

Do not sell an AI transformation. Show them where leads are leaking.

---

## 2. Bundle two outcomes into one clear offer

Example from the experiment: **Local Lead and Reputation Engine.**

Promise:

> We help local businesses turn missed calls and new inquiries into booked appointments, then automatically turn happy customers into five-star reviews without adding work for their team.

Include:

- Instant text-back for missed calls
- Immediate follow-up for website and social inquiries
- A simple booking or callback flow
- Appointment reminders
- Automated review requests after a completed job
- A monthly scorecard
- Done-for-you setup and ongoing management

Guarantee something you control. Seven-day setup. Setup fee refunded if you miss it.

Sell the business result. Keep the AI in the fulfillment layer.

---

## 3. Make the pitch simple enough to say at the front desk

One line. Then three steps.

1. Missed-call text-back and instant follow-up so every lead gets a fast response.
2. A booking flow and reminders so more people actually schedule.
3. After each job, automated review requests.

If it takes five minutes to explain, it is still too complicated.

---

## 4. Run the technology behind the scenes

One platform (GoHighLevel or equivalent).

Operator configures: missed-call text-back, shared inbox, follow-up, booking link, review requests.

Client keeps two jobs: respond to hot leads, mark jobs complete.

If they already have a sophisticated CRM they actually use, skip them at the beginning.

Positioning: we don't replace your current systems. We add a managed response and review layer around them.

---

## 5. Price the pilot before you have proof

Founding (proposed, not validated):

- $750 setup
- $500 / month
- Cancel after 90 days
- Collect setup before you build

After proof:

- $1,500–$2,500 setup
- $750–$1,250 / month

Do not start with pay-per-lead. Attribution fights before you have a baseline will eat you.

Localise the currency. Say the number out loud. If you stumble, it is the wrong number.

---

## 6. Build the minimum sales asset

Name it. One promise. Plain language. A working consult button. Three steps. What is in the offer.

Ninety percent is enough. Do not spend three weeks on a brand before you know anyone wants it.

---

## 7. Find prospects based on visible leaks

Independently operated service businesses near you.

- Strong Google reviews
- Weak or missing website / booking path
- Owner-operated feel
- A physical location you can visit

Public signals only. A missing website is a prospecting signal, not proof they lack a CRM.

Order them as a practical route. Closest to farthest from your neighborhood.

The insight is not "businesses that need AI." It is businesses with visible demand, leaking money in the current process.

---

## 8. The 15-minute assessment

Used as the thing you can offer at the desk.

| Clock | Move |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 | Walk me through what happens after a new lead comes in. |
| 2–6 | Find the leak. Who owns it. How long. How often. What a miss is worth. |
| 6–10 | Score 1–5 on frequency, cost, ease. |
| 10–13 | One concrete fix. Not an AI transformation. |
| 13–15 | Offer the paid assessment. |

Weekly numbers: doors, owner conversations, assessments booked, paid assessments sold.

A later model from Corey: free mini assessment → paid assessment ($1k–$2k) → concierge retainer ($1k–$2k / month). Every stage sells the next one. Boundaries are the offer.

---

## 9. Experiment to operating business

1. Pick one niche with frequent inbound calls and valuable appointments.
2. Speak with five owners before making the offer more complicated.
3. Confirm how missed calls, inquiries, booking, and reviews work today.
4. Sell one transparent founding-client pilot.
5. Collect the setup fee before building the full system.
6. Install only the workflows required for that client.
7. Track response time, conversations, bookings, review requests, reviews generated.
8. Send a simple monthly scorecard.
9. Document every setup step as a reusable checklist.
10. Turn the first verified result into proof, then raise the price.

Step two is the one the software cannot do.

---

## 10. Final

AI compressed the blank page. It did not walk through the door.

Walk through the door. Ask better questions. Close the first client. Deliver the result. Turn that result into a repeatable business.
